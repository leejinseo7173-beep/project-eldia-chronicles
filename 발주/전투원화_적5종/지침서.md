```
16-bit JRPG pixel art sprite, SNES era, chunky retro style,
full body, 4-head-tall proportions with a BIG head, thick black outline,
LARGE visible pixel blocks, low detail, limited palette with 2-3 shading steps,
```

## 4. 종별 — 외형 블록 + 공격·시전 문구

### 1) 해골 병사 (돌격형 — 뼈 검을 마구 휘두르는 소모품 보병)

```
undead skeleton soldier, bleached white bone body, hollow black eye sockets,
rusty dented gray breastplate and tattered dark rags,
jagged bone sword, hunched aggressive posture
```

- **attack0**: `raising the bone sword high overhead with both hands`
- **attack1**: `mid wild downward swing, blade sweeping toward the left`
- **attack2**: `impact follow-through, blade low to the left, body lunging in` ★가장 중요
- **attack3**: `recovering stance, sword dragged back up`
- **cast**: `raising the sword and rattling in a battle cry, jaw wide open`

### 2) 해골 궁수 (사수형 — 뒷열에서 아군 유리몸을 노리는 저격수)

```
undead skeleton archer, bleached white bone body, hollow black eye sockets,
tattered gray hood and quiver of black arrows on the back,
crooked dark longbow
```

- **attack0**: `nocking an arrow onto the bowstring, bow lowered` (화살 OK)
- **attack1**: `full draw, bowstring pulled to the jaw, aiming left` (화살 OK)
- **attack2**: `just released the string, bow arm extended left, NO arrow anywhere` ★화살 없음
- **attack3**: `lowering the bow, reaching back to the quiver`
- **cast**: `aiming steeply upward at full draw` (강사 — 파워샷용)

### 3) 암흑 주술사 (지원형 — 아군을 되살리고 축복하는 뒷열 우선 목표)

```
dark shaman in ragged purple hooded robe with black lining,
gaunt gray skin, glowing purple eyes inside the hood,
crooked wooden staff topped with a purple fetish skull
```

- **attack0**: `raising the staff to aim at a target on the left`
- **attack1**: `gathering power, staff pulled back, robe sleeves fluttering`
- **attack2**: `thrusting the staff toward the left, spell released, empty air in front` ★구체 없음
- **attack3**: `lowering the staff, hunching back down`
- **cast**: `both arms spread wide in a dark ritual, staff held vertical, head thrown back` (치유·축복)

### 4) 그림자 살수 (교활형 — 유리몸만 노리는 암습자)

```
shadow assassin in skin-tight black cloth and dark gray half-mask,
only sharp pale eyes visible, short black hood scarf,
twin curved daggers held in reverse grip, low crouching sneaky posture
```

- **attack0**: `crouching low, daggers crossed ready to pounce`
- **attack1**: `lunging forward, one dagger slashing toward the left`
- **attack2**: `deep stab follow-through, both daggers driven low-left, body fully extended` ★가장 중요
- **attack3**: `springing back to a crouch, daggers re-crossed`
- **cast**: `melting into a crouch, cloak wrapped around, half dissolved into black smoke` (암습 준비)

### 5) 망령 기사 (수호형 — 아군 공격을 대신 받는 물리 벽)

```
spectral wraith knight, translucent gray ghost-flame body inside
heavy battered dark full plate armor, glowing pale blue eye slits,
long cavalry lance and a tall battered kite shield, bulky silhouette
```

- **attack0**: `lance couched back, shield braced forward`
- **attack1**: `mid powerful lance thrust toward the left`
- **attack2**: `full extension pierce, lance tip low-left, shield tucked` ★가장 중요
- **attack3**: `withdrawing the lance, shield re-raised`
- **cast**: `slamming the shield down and roaring, ghost-flame flaring up` (도발 포효)
